Chapter 2: The Birth of icmPulse (and the end of playing Indiana Jones)

Woken up by a rooster at 7 AM, you suddenly remember you were supposed to submit a leave request... Instead of digging through dusty Excel sheets, meet the new era of our company information hub.

#Timesheets #LeavePlanner #WhoIsIt? #Onboarding #TravelExpenses #TheWall #TeamsAndMedia #PulsAI

Woken up by a rooster crowing at 7 AM, you suddenly remember you were supposed to submit a leave request today — sound familiar?

Now you just need to dress up like Indiana Jones, boot up your computer, and set off on an expedition to find the right Excel spreadsheet with the current request template. The one that isn't "leave_form_FINAL_v3_reallyfinal.xlsx" from 2023. The one your manager will actually accept without asking "wait, is this the right version?"

You finally find it. You fill it in. You email it to HR, CC your manager, CC yourself just in case, and pray to the productivity gods that nobody replies-all with "please use the new template" three minutes later.

But even if you win that battle — the war isn't over. What if someone in your team has already booked the exact same slot? How many days of paid leave do you even have left this year? Is that number in your head accurate, or is it wishful thinking left over from January?

We're a company of 30 people, not 3,000 — we shouldn't need a spreadsheet archaeology degree just to take a Friday off.

Out of precisely these small, daily absurdities came the idea for icmPulse (or simply Pulse).

Module 01 • Timesheets & Attendance
Speed Demon (Bad)

Timesheet: Only the Things That Matter

Do you remember how our colleague managed to injure his finger? He doesn't even remember how it happened... But I do. It was a Friday. You know the vibe: that specific, high-stakes moment when everyone simultaneously rushes to submit their timesheets. His finger probably slipped between the keys at the exact millisecond his other finger, twitching nervously like a pneumatic drill, decided to smash the same letter. A small thing... It didn't have to end this way... It’s too late for tears now, and we aren't here to point fingers (pun intended).

But can we prevent similar incidents in the future?
Absolutely.

No more wrestling with spreadsheet cells, guessing project codes, or playing detective with your own workweek. We stripped away the bureaucratic noise and the Friday panic.

  • One-Click Templates: Your most frequently used projects and tasks populate with a single click. Because you shouldn't need a PhD in Excel macros just to log 8 hours of work (and you definitely don't need two fully functional fingers).
  • Automatic Verification: The system acts as your intelligent safety net. It catches missing hours, overlapping entries, and invalid codes before you hit submit. The era of "please fix this" bounce-back emails is officially over.
  • Weekly Overview: A clear, functional view of your logged weekly hours, alongside total team hours for a specific client. No more guessing if the team is over or under budget.
  • Transparent Stats & Automated Reporting: Real-time visibility into your time allocation, paired with automated weekly reminders. You will never again face the dreaded "end-of-month timesheet avalanche."

Log your time, hit submit, and get back to the actual work.
Speed Demon mode: activated. Fingers: safe.

Module 02 • Leave Requests & Absence Map
HIStory (1995)

Leave Requests: Click, Fill, Submit and Forget

Let me share a HIStory — almost true, from one of my previous companies. It was mid-August. Three team members independently submitted leave requests for the same critical week. Nobody checked. Nobody knew. The first warning sign? A client call with the question: "Where is your entire team?" Cue the panic. Cue the awkward conversations. Cue the manager playing referee between three equally valid vacation plans.

It was a total HIStory-level chaos — the kind that becomes office legend. Nobody wanted to be the bad guy saying "you can't go." Nobody checked the spreadsheet (because there wasn't one). And so, history repeated itself.

But what if you could see the future before it happens?
What if conflicts were impossible by design?

We propose centralized leave management. A lightweight digital form improves information flow and instantly reveals team availability for planned days off. No more guessing. No more conflicts. No more HIStory repeating itself.

  • Visual Timeline: See who's out and when — color-coded blocks on a clear timeline. No more spreadsheet archaeology or "wait, who's on leave next Tuesday?"
  • Instant Team Availability: Before you even submit your request, you see who else is already booked. Conflicts? Prevented by design.
  • Lightweight Digital Form: Click, fill, submit, forget. Three steps max. Your manager gets notified automatically. No emails. No CC chains. No "did you get my request?" follow-ups.
  • Centralized Management: One source of truth for all leave requests. Approved, pending, rejected — all visible in real-time. No more "I thought you approved it" conversations.
  • Annual Overview: Track your remaining days, see public holidays, and plan ahead without the guesswork. Your leave balance, finally accurate.

On the HIStory album, Michael Jackson compiled his greatest hits.
Our leave calendar is also a history — but one we control, not one that controls us.

Module 04 • Onboarding Checklist
Man in the Mirror (Bad)

Onboarding: No Longer a Treasure Hunt for Your Own Laptop

Every new hire's first week used to look the same: a stack of forced-smile introductions, a login that doesn't work until Wednesday, and a mystery about who actually owns the Wi-Fi password. First days shouldn't feel like an escape room.

With icmPulse, day one gets a script. A single checklist walks every new joiner through equipment, accounts, and the people they'll actually need — no more "ask around and hope."

  • A single, trackable checklist: equipment, accounts, policies, and paperwork — all in one place, tick by tick.
  • An assigned buddy and manager appear automatically, straight from the People module — no cold-emailing strangers.
  • Day-30 and day-90 check-ins are scheduled on their own, so nobody gets forgotten after week one.

Module 05 • Travel & Expense Claims
Stranger in Moscow (HIStory)

Travel & Expenses: Making Peace With Receipts

You land after a business trip with a jacket pocket full of crumpled receipts, half of them in a currency you can't quite remember converting, and a vague sense that the per diem rules changed again while you were gone.

icmPulse turns that pocket lint into a two-minute job: snap a photo of the receipt, pick the trip, and the system pulls the right allowance rates automatically — no more guessing whether it's €153 or €135 a night.

  • Mobile receipt capture: photograph it once, and it's attached, categorized, and ready for approval.
  • Built-in policy rates: subsistence, mileage, and daily allowances calculated for you, not looked up in a PDF.
  • A clear approval trail: submit, track status, get reimbursed — no more "did you get my email?" follow-ups.

Module 07 • Teams & Media Integrations
Rock With You (Off the Wall)

Teams & Media: One Notification Instead of Five Tabs

Before icmPulse, "staying informed" meant living across five open tabs: your inbox, Teams, the shared drive, the wiki nobody updates, and whichever spreadsheet someone linked in a message you scrolled past.

Instead of asking everyone to check one more system, Pulse comes to where people already are. A leave request gets approved in Teams. A new company announcement drops straight into the channel it belongs in. One source of truth, delivered wherever you happen to be looking.

  • Microsoft Teams notifications for approvals, reminders, and Wall posts — no separate app to check.
  • A synced media library so photos from the last team event don't quietly die in someone's phone.
  • Single sign-on: one login for Pulse, Teams, and everything connected to it.
